## Open units of work

### DW-01 — The editor's Enter at the end or start of a block skips source ranking, so the text order depends on the client ids

- **Area:** CRDT correctness
- **Severity:** medium
- **Effort:** M
- **Location:** `src/lib/events/beforeInputCommands.ts:310`. Enter at the end goes through `insertBlockAfter` (:310) and Enter at the start through `insertBlockBefore` (:316); only a mid-block Enter reaches `splitBlock` (:323). The path runs `prepareSibling` (`block.utils.ts:224-230`), then the facade `insertBlocks`, which ranks with random `ranksFor` (`edytor-doc.ts:1912-1923`).
- **Failure:** Alice presses Enter at the end of 'hello world' and types 'foo'. Bob concurrently presses Enter after 'hello'. The result is 'hello' / 'foo' / ' world' on 24 of 48 client-id pairs, which no serial order produces.
  - Insert-after on p ‖ Backspace-lift of the first item of the list below gives 'p a n b' on 24 of 48 pairs. That contradicts editor-delete-contract.md:522-523 ("reads the paragraph's pieces, then the item") and :541-547.
  - The sweep row `cw01-order-sweep.test.ts:208`, labelled "Enter at the end", drives `splitBlock(X,11)`, which the editor never issues for that key.
  - Duplicate and the slash/+ insert share the random path.
  - Every replica converges and no text is lost.
- **Fix:** Rank inserts at index+1 with the pieces-side source rank a split at the block's end would get: `pieceRanks(prev, displayLength(prev), n)`, part 0. An insert before a block ranks on that block's side. Alternatively, route Enter at the end or start through facade `splitBlock` at the edge offset. Add sweep rows for the editor-shaped op (insert after X) ‖ a mid split, and for insert-after ‖ a first-item lift. If the code is left as is, narrow contract :522-523 and :541-547 and concurrent-editing.mdx:118-119 to mid-block splits, and name Enter at the end or start, Duplicate and the + insert as residuals.
- **Done when:** both repros hold the serial order on `clientPairs(240)`, and the :208 row drives the editor's actual path. If the scope is narrowed instead, the docs name these gestures as residuals.

### DW-02 — A second structural gesture by one peer before sync sorts after the other peer's source-ranked blocks

- **Area:** CRDT correctness
- **Severity:** medium
- **Effort:** M
- **Location:** `src/lib/crdt/placement/model.ts:480`. The base of `sourceRank` (:469-480) is `rankBetween(left, right)` over the gap the author currently sees.
- **Failure:** A peer's second gesture in a gap bounded by its own new block takes a fresh level-0 midpoint, which sorts after every block the other peer ranked from the original gap.
  - (a) [X 'hello world', ul[a,b]]: Ada presses Enter after 'hello' and then after ' wo' ‖ Bob Backspace-lifts a. The result reads 'hello',' wo','a','rld' on 48 of 48 pairs (it was 33 of 48 at 38d5be1).
  - (b) [p, ul[a,b,c,d]]: Ada unlists a, then b ‖ Bob outdents c. The result reads 'pacbd' on 24 of 48 pairs (it was 13 of 48).
  - The single-gesture controls fail 0 of 48. Both results contradict editor-delete-contract.md:513-525 and concurrent-editing.mdx:175 ("on every client-id assignment").
  - The sweeps run only one gesture per peer, so they cannot catch this. The documents converge and no text is lost.
- **Fix:** Anchor the base on the original source gap. When the gap sits next to a block the same peer placed there with a source rank, extend that block's rank as a prefix instead of taking a fresh midpoint. Add sweep rows where one peer makes two gestures before sync: two mid splits ‖ a lift, two lifts ‖ an outdent, and a lift then Enter ‖ an outdent. Otherwise, limit the doc claims to one gesture per peer and list multi-gesture sequences as a residual.
- **Done when:** repros (a) and (b) keep the serial order on `clientPairs(240)` and the multi-gesture rows are green. If the scope is limited instead, the contract and concurrent-editing.mdx name the residual.

### DW-03 — Mod+Enter is a raw splitBlock at the end of the caret's Text node, skipping the Enter role rules

- **Area:** Editing behaviors
- **Severity:** medium
- **Effort:** S
- **Location:** `src/lib/session/bindings.ts:261`
- **Failure:**
  - Callout 'C' with children {k, k2}, caret at the end of 'C', Mod+Enter gives [callout 'C' (no children), paragraph '' {k, k2}, z]. The body leaves the container. A quote behaves the same way. Plain Enter at the same spot correctly gives callout 'C' {'', k, k2}.
  - 'Ping ' @Ada ' about the release' with the caret at offset 2 gives ['Ping '] and [@Ada ' about the release']. The atom and the rest of the line move down.
  - This contradicts internal contract K11 ("splits at the end of the block"). The public wording at hotkeys.mdx:21 ("the end of the caret's text") hides it. Tests cover only 'Hello|', the to-do and the toggle. One undo recovers the document.
- **Fix:** Place the caret at the end of `lineOf(block).at(-1)` and run `runIntent('insertParagraph')` inside one `dispatcher.run`, keeping the DETAILS toggle and to-do branches. Reword hotkeys.mdx:21 to say "at the end of the block's line".
- **Done when:** DOM rows for callout {k}, quote {k} and 'Ping @Ada …' with the caret before the atom pass. A container keeps its body and gets a new first child, and the atom stays on its line.

### DW-04 — Enter over a block selection empties and keeps the first selected block; onDeleteSelectedBlocks reports blocks that survive

- **Area:** Editing behaviors (medium). The same root cause is reported in API ease of use (low).
- **Severity:** medium
- **Effort:** S
- **Location:** `src/lib/events/beforeInputCommands.ts:253`. `insertParagraph` calls `keepsSelectedBlocks` and then `replaceSelectionWithCollapsedTarget`, a text-range delete. Typing, Shift+Enter and composition use `replaceSelectedBlocksWithEmptyBlock` (flow.slot, `replaceSelection.ts:221-238`).
- **Failure:** with no veto:
  - [a, b] selected: Enter gives [a '' (same id), new '', z].
  - A heading stays an empty heading.
  - A checked to-do becomes an empty paragraph and no new block appears.
  - A closed toggle keeps its children.
  - A code block survives intact, but the hook reports it as removed.
  - The documented select-all ladder (Mod+A, Mod+A, Enter) wipes the paragraph's text.
  - writing-plugins.mdx:193, plugins.ts:148-153 and migration.mdx:137/200 say each such gesture "replaces the blocks with what it inserts". A plugin that frees per-block state in the hook frees it for a block that still exists, and undo does not restore that state.
  - Correction to the API finding: the first block keeps its children only when it is selected alone. When a parent and its next sibling are selected together, the child is deleted with the range.
- **Fix:** Choose one rule for every kind.
  - Option A (Notion): Enter leaves the block selection and puts the caret at the end of the first selected block's line, with no document change and no hook call.
  - Option B: route Enter through `replaceSelectedBlocksWithEmptyBlock` like typing, with the hook first, then place one more empty block.
  - In either case, run the hook only when blocks are actually removed, and align writing-plugins.mdx:193, plugins.ts:148-153 and migration.mdx:137/200.
- **Done when:** DOM rows cover a heading, a checked to-do, a closed toggle, a code block and the select-all ladder, each without a veto. In every row the hook's reported ids match the blocks actually removed, and the docs describe the behaviour that ships.

### DW-05 — sourceRank lets one client mint the same rank twice in a gap, leaving tied siblings

- **Area:** CRDT correctness
- **Severity:** low
- **Effort:** S
- **Location:** `src/lib/crdt/placement/model.ts:470`. The part comes from `pieceRanks` as `[at - displayLength(id), i]` (`edytor-doc.ts:1705-1727`). The degenerate-gap fallback `ranksAt` (`model.ts:813-815`) fills with `left`.
- **Failure:**
  1. Ada splits X at its end and types 'one'.
  2. Bob deletes that line.
  3. Ada splits X again at the same distance from the end and types 'two'.
  4. Bob presses undo.

  After this, `slotOf(b_m1).rank === slotOf(b_z2).rank` on all 6 id pairs tried. Ada's next Enter at the end of 'one' joins the tie, and the new block's position depends on its random id ('three|one|two' or 'one|two|three'). The documents converge and no text is lost. The finder's exact 'hello world' wording does not tie; the tie needs the same distance from the end, which is the natural Enter-at-end case.

- **Fix:** Add a per-document monotonic segment to the part (the doc clock or a nonce at prepare time), or make `sourceRank`/`pieceRanks` descend when a sibling already holds the computed rank.
- **Done when:** a split, delete, re-split and undo row asserts distinct ranks, and a following Enter lands after the line where it was pressed on every id.

## Refuted

No proposed finding was refuted outright. Verification changed or narrowed these claims, and the reviewers deliberately left these observations out of the score:

- **Docs migration heading (DW-06):** proposed as medium, confirmed at **low**. The correct bullets are on the same page, and only next.0 was ever served.
- **API "children kept" claim (DW-04):** holds only when the parent is selected alone. With a parent and its next sibling selected, the child is deleted with the range.
- **Tie repro (DW-05):** the finder's exact 'hello world' split-at-5 wording does not tie. The tie needs the same distance from the end. The finding holds in that form.
- **Hook runs twice on typing or Enter (API, Code):** a jsdom harness artifact. The helper yields to the keydown's timeout before the beforeinput arrives. Real Chromium runs the hook once per gesture.
- **Untrusted `deleteByDrag` skips `onDeleteSelectedBlocks` (Code):** not reachable by a user. `onBeforeInput.ts:521` prevents a trusted deleteByDrag, and internal drag-move is unsupported.
- **Not counted as findings (Code):** the four call sites apply slightly different guards before `keepsSelectedBlocks`. A vetoed paste or typing reports `dispatcher.last.operation` as 'deleteSelectedBlocks'; the docs promise only `refused`.
- **Carried refutations, not reopened (Collaboration):** a readonly view never dials; the actor-scoped persistName keeps the raw `server` (matches persistence.mdx:36); the BroadcastChannel is shared when persist:false (persistence.mdx:97).
